# Rennrad-Routen Sulcis-Iglesiente im Umkreis von 0–50 km um Punta Trettu (San Giovanni Suergiu / Sant'Antioco)

> Method note: these notes come from about 20 searches. WebFetch failed on every page (DNS "ENOTFOUND" for komoot.com, gps-tour.info and sardegnaturismo.it; the proxy returned 403 for curl). So the figures below are from search-result snippets and the search tool's summaries of them, not from reading the full pages. They are user or operator figures and were not checked against a GPX file. komoot numbers differ between versions of the same tour. Strava segments could not be found or opened at all.

## 1. Loops starting at Punta Trettu / San Giovanni Suergiu (Sant'Antioco, San Pietro, Giba–Porto Pino, Tratalias, Santadi, Carbonia, Iglesias–Nebida–Buggerru)

### Takeaway
The best-documented road loops are on Isola di Sant'Antioco. You can ride straight from San Giovanni Suergiu over the causeway and the coast road, about 60–69 km with about 400–450 m of climbing, or ride shorter island loops of 29–41 km. Isola di San Pietro (ferry from Calasetta) has a hilly loop of about 42 km and 610 m. The tougher terrain is in the north, in the Iglesiente: the Nebida–Masua–Buggerru coast and the Genna Bogai pass. The flat-to-rolling inland area (Tratalias, Giba, Porto Pino, Santadi) has thin road-bike sources.

### Cited Findings
**Isola di Sant'Antioco (directly from Punta Trettu)**
- komoot "Giro dell'isola di Sant'Antioco da San Giovanni Suergiu – giro ad anello": about 68.7 km, about 440 m of climbing, about 4 h 19 min. Its highlight is the Tratalias–Sant'Antioco cycle path, called the most beautiful section. A second version, "Tour dell'isola di Sant'Antioco da San Giovanni Suergiu", is about 67 km with about 390 m, rated intermediate. komoot warns that some sections may be unpaved — [komoot Smarttour e996965398](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/smarttour/e996965398/giro-dell-isola-di-sant-antioco-da-san-giovanni-suergiu-giro-ad-anello); [komoot Smarttour e1013194383](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/smarttour/e1013194383/tour-dell-isola-di-sant-antioco-da-san-giovanni-suergiu)
- The official road-bike route of the Comune di Calasetta, "Periplo dell'Isola di Sant'Antioco", is 40.6 km, designed by Anfibia. It starts at Calasetta harbour, runs along the NW coast past Borgo Cussorgia, goes through Sant'Antioco town and into the hills (woods and macchia), returns to the sea at Cala Sapone and comes back via Cala Lunga. It is rated "medium". An optional short gravel detour leads to Mangiabarche (skip it on a road bike) — [GPS-Tour.info 118513](https://www.gps-tour.info/118513)
- komoot loops on the island: "Punta di Vacca – Calasetta Old Town and Cliffs" from Calasetta, about 29 km and 170 m, intermediate, mostly paved. "Cliffs of Calasetta – Calasetta Old Town and Cliffs" from Sant'Antioco, about 37 km and 460 m, rated hard. "Calasetta Old Town and Cliffs – Cala Sapone", about 34.6 km and 430 m; a user notes a sharp descent towards Sant'Antioco after Monte S'Arraigraxiu. "Calasetta Marina – Lonely street with good surface loop" from Maladroxia, about 60 km, a road-bike tour on mostly well-surfaced roads. One user describes the Punta di Vacca section as essentially a double track — [komoot Smarttour 3977980 and highlights](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/3977980); [komoot Highlight 5013242](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5013242)
- komoot road-bike guides for the area: [Road-bike routes around Sant'Antioco](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/guide/609778/fantastici-percorsi-per-bici-da-corsa-nei-dintorni-di-santu-antiogu-sant-antioco) (one loop is about 59.4 km); [Road-bike routes around Calasetta](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/guide/622171/fantastici-percorsi-per-bici-da-corsa-nei-dintorni-di-cadesedda-calasetta) (one loop is about 55.4 km)
- komoot loop from Carbonia to Isola di Sant'Antioco: about 63 km, about 240 m, medium. 34.1 km is asfalt, and rollers and wind are mentioned — [komoot guide Sant'Antioco (search snippet)](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/guide/609778/fantastici-percorsi-per-bici-da-corsa-nei-dintorni-di-santu-antiogu-sant-antioco)
- Tour operator 10 Adventures: Sant'Antioco island loop of 42 km and 480 m — [10adventures](https://cms.10adventures.com/tour/sardinia-cycling-tour)

**Isola di San Pietro / Carloforte (ferry from Calasetta)**
- komoot "Giro dell'isola di San Pietro e Carloforte": about 42 km and about 610 m, rated hard, mostly paved. Capo Sandalo lighthouse is at about km 21.4 — [komoot Smarttour e996937269](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/smarttour/e996937269/giro-dell-isola-di-san-pietro-e-carloforte-giro-ad-anello)
- CAI Cagliari: from Carloforte to Capo Sandalo is "about 11 km of asphalt road with long climbs and some fast descents". The road is the SP 104. The CAI full loop (47 km, 800 m) includes bad dirt tracks and is an MTB route. Ferries run from Portovesme or from Calasetta — [CAI Cagliari 2016 (PDF)](https://www.caicagliari.it/cicloescursionismo/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2018/03/2016_05_15-Descrizione-S.-Pietro.pdf); [CAI Cagliari 2025 (PDF)](https://www.caicagliari.it/cicloescursionismo/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/09/2025_09_20-Descrizione-cicloescursione-Carloforte-c-var.pdf)
- Bikemap "Carbonia–Carloforte–S. Antioco–Calasetta–Carbonia": about 109.8 km, crossing both islands with the ferry — [Bikemap 4035539](https://www.bikemap.net/it/r/4035539/)

**Tratalias / Giba / Porto Pino / Sant'Anna Arresi (south)**
- 10 Adventures, stage Sant'Antioco → Porto Pino via Tratalias (old church and village), through vineyards and countryside: 35 km and 220 m — [10adventures](https://cms.10adventures.com/tour/sardinia-cycling-tour); the same structure appears at [TourRadar 212261](https://www.tourradar.com/t/212261)
- Level Voyageur (family tour): Sant'Antioco → Sulcis plain → Tratalias → Sant'Anna Arresi/Teulada, about 25 km; the next day is 36 km along the coast to Chia — [levelovoyageur](https://www.levelovoyageur.com/en/trip/southern-sardinia-by-bike-with-the-family/)
- Sardegna Turismo: a comfortable cycle path runs from San Giovanni Suergiu to Carbonia. From Tratalias the route continues towards Giba and Sant'Anna Arresi, then turns onto the path to the Porto Pino dunes — [sardegnaturismo node 253121](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/it/node/253121)
- komoot guide for road-bike routes around Tratalias (no figures could be read) — [komoot guide 570712](https://www.komoot.com/guide/570712/road-cycling-routes-around-tratalias)
- komoot "Anello della Costa del Sud", in the guide for southern Sardinia: about 48 km and about 620 m, intermediate, mostly paved. The Costa del Sud (Teulada–Chia) is at the edge of the 50 km radius — [komoot guide 62409](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/guide/62409/fantastici-percorsi-per-bici-da-corsa-nella-sardegna-del-sud)

**Iglesiente (north, about 25–45 km from Punta Trettu)**
- Genna Bogai pass (549 m) on the SS126 between Iglesias and Fluminimaggiore. komoot marks it for road cycling. The climb from Iglesias is about 10 km and 380 m; from Fluminimaggiore about 12 km and 500 m; the descent is about 12 km. Users mention many hairpins, "light traffic", and free-roaming sheep and cattle. It is tiring in the heat — [komoot Highlight 3911409](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/3911409); [komoot Highlight 2578089](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/2578089)
- Loops from Iglesias over the pass: about 54 km and 990 m via Su Zurfuru (hard); "From Iglesias through the mines of Su Zurfuru and Monteponi" about 75 km — [komoot Smarttour e996970556](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e996970556/from-iglesias-through-the-mines-of-su-zurfuru-and-monteponi-ring-tour); komoot guide [Mountain passes in Sud Sardegna](https://www.komoot.com/guide/3892066/mountain-passes-in-sud-sardegna)
- A motorcycle site describes the SS126 Fluminimaggiore–Iglesias as very twisty, with blind corners and high crash barriers in places (2009 review, may be dated) — [bestbikingroads](https://bestbikingroads.com/motorcycle-roads/italy/sardegna/ride/ss126-fluminimaggiore-iglesias)
- Nebida–Masua–Buggerru coast: komoot loop "Steep Ascent Near Sa Rocha – View of Buggerru loop from Nebida", 77.1 km and 1,690 m, rated hard, mostly paved. Highlight "Panoramic View of Masua", rated 5.0 from 12 reviews, with a 2023 tip about the view after the climb. A "Curved Ascent near Buggerru" climb leaves Buggerru. A tip says the road between the mountains towards Nebida is a fast descent — [komoot Highlight 6073031](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/6073031); [komoot Highlight 4136774](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/4136774); [komoot Highlight 4136768](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/4136768)
- SP83 Iglesias → Nebida: in 2020 the Iglesias council (M5S motion) said it is the most used access to Nebida and has several subsidence spots near the village entrance that heavy traffic was making worse — [Comune di Iglesias, Interrogazione 2020 (PDF)](https://www.comune.iglesias.ca.it/.galleries/doc-consiglio-comunale/interrogazione-Garau-25047-2020-strada-prov.-83-cc-27.07.2020.pdf)
- The 2nd stage of the Giro della Sardegna 2026 rides Montevecchio–Guspini–Arbus–Portixeddu–Buggerru–Masua (Porto Flavia)–Nebida and finishes in Carbonia. This confirms the coast road is a race road — [sardegnaturismo node 288061](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/node/288061)
- Bikemap route from Iglesias towards Fluminimaggiore: about 61 km, of which only about 10 km is tagged "quiet road" and the rest mixed track and road — [Bikemap 17880739](https://www.bikemap.net/en/r/17880739/)

**Santadi / Nuxis / Pantaleo / Villaperuccio / Narcao**
- Pantaleo state forest (4,231 ha, partly in the Nuxis municipality): the CAI routes there are on dirt roads (MTB) and not suitable for road bikes. The Monte Nieddu di Nuxis climb averages about 15% and is mostly unpaved — [CAI Pantaleo (PDF)](https://www.caicagliari.it/cicloescursionismo/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2018/02/2017_01_14-Descrizione-Pantaleo.pdf); [CAI Nuxis (PDF)](https://www.caicagliari.it/cicloescursionismo/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2016/02/2016_02_07-Descrizione-Nuxis.pdf)
- The komoot bikepacking "Sulcis Loop" (240 km, 4,500 m, 3 days) is an MTB route, not for road bikes — [komoot collection 1869656](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/collection/1869656/sulcis-in-fundo-sulcis-loop)

### Inferences
- The most practical "front door" routes from Punta Trettu are: (a) Sant'Antioco island, 40–69 km, low climbing, wind as the main factor; (b) Sant'Antioco plus the ferry to San Pietro, 80–110 km with more climbing; (c) south via Tratalias/Giba to Porto Pino, flat, 50–80 km out and back; (d) the Iglesiente for climbs: Nebida–Masua–Buggerru plus Genna Bogai, about 100–140 km out and back from Punta Trettu or by car to Iglesias.
- Elevation totals for the same routes differ a lot between sources (e.g. Sant'Antioco loop 390–480 m), so do not rely on single figures.
- Santadi–Nuxis–Villaperuccio–Narcao–Perdaxius–Monte Pranu (Tratalias): no road-bike sources found. From the map these are rural provincial roads that should suit an inland loop, but that is unverified and the road numbers are not confirmed.

### Gaps
- No full komoot, Strava or Bikemap page could be opened, so no complete GPX details, road numbers (e.g. for the Sant'Antioco island roads or Giba–Masainas) or recent comments.
- Nothing road-specific found for Monte Pranu, Santadi, Nuxis, Villaperuccio, Narcao, Perdaxius, Domusnovas, Villamassargia or the edges of Monte Linas.
- Not confirmed whether the Masua–Buggerru coast section is also numbered SP83.

## 2. Busy roads to avoid (SS126, SS195, SS130) and quiet alternatives

### Takeaway
Sources specific to traffic are almost absent. Only for Genna Bogai on the SS126 north of Iglesias do users report "light traffic". The road-safety solutions documented in the sources are the cycle paths Carbonia–San Giovanni Suergiu (built) and San Giovanni Suergiu–Tratalias–Sant'Antioco (partly in planning or tendering).

### Cited Findings
- Genna Bogai section of the SS126 (Iglesias–Fluminimaggiore): users report "light traffic" but livestock on the road — [komoot Highlight 3911409](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/3911409)
- During the Giro di Sardegna, all junctions in the Carbonia municipality, including the roundabouts on the SS126, were staffed by police. This at least shows the SS126 is a main axis through Carbonia — [Unione Sarda: Carbonia aspetta il Giro di Sardegna](https://www.unionesarda.it/news-sardegna/agenda/carbonia-aspetta-il-giro-di-sardegna-vftht9kp)
- Cycle-path network: Carbonia–San Giovanni Suergiu is "already built". San Giovanni Suergiu–Sant'Antioco was "in tendering" according to a project document from the Comune di Tratalias (uploaded 2024, but the dates inside point to about 2017). The path is 2.5 m wide with a 1 m bridle path alongside — [Comune di Tratalias, Progetto Esecutivo (PDF)](https://comune.tratalias.su.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Progetto-Esecutivo-Allegati-3.pdf)
- komoot calls the Tratalias–Sant'Antioco cycle path the most beautiful section of the island loop from San Giovanni Suergiu — [komoot Smarttour e996965398](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/smarttour/e996965398/giro-dell-isola-di-sant-antioco-da-san-giovanni-suergiu-giro-ad-anello)
- The national cycle route ("Ciclovia nazionale") leaves out the Sulcis-Iglesiente (Iglesias, Carbonia, San Giovanni Suergiu, Gonnesa, Sant'Antioco, Narcao …), and mayors have protested. San Giovanni Suergiu is described as a hub for many cycle paths under construction — [Unione Sarda](https://www.unionesarda.it/news-sardegna/sulcis-iglesiente/il-sulcis-iglesiente-tagliato-fuori-dalla-ciclovia-nazionale-gzjae6wn)
- Corriere dello Sport recommends a road bike for the inland routes along the SS126 in the Costa Verde (Piscinas, Ingurtosu, Montevecchio), and MTB or gravel bikes for the dirt tracks — [Corriere dello Sport 2024](https://www.corrieredellosport.it/news/attualit/2024/02/19-122658841/pedalando_alla_scoperta_della_sardegna)

### Inferences
- **These are inferences from road classification and general knowledge, not backed by sources:** the SS126 between Sant'Antioco, San Giovanni Suergiu, Carbonia and Iglesias and the SS130 Iglesias–Cagliari (a dual carriageway) are the main traffic axes and likely busy. The SS195 to Teulada and Cagliari is likely busy near Cagliari but probably moderate in the deep south. The SS126 north of Iglesias over Genna Bogai, by contrast, is quiet according to user reports.
- The causeway from the mainland to Isola di Sant'Antioco is the bottleneck. The cycle path (if it is finished) is the preferred alternative, but its status is unclear.

### Gaps
- No traffic counts, no Strava heatmap evaluation, and no forum posts on the SS126 Carbonia–Sant'Antioco, SS195 or SS130 from a cyclist's view.
- Whether the San Giovanni Suergiu–Sant'Antioco cycle path is finished as of 2026 was not confirmed.

## 3. Known climbs and segments, road surface, closures

### Takeaway
The documented climbs are Genna Bogai (SS126, 10 km and 380 m from Iglesias; 12 km and 500 m from Fluminimaggiore), the Nebida–Masua–Buggerru coast climbs (Masua viewpoint, Sa Rocha ramp, the climb out of Buggerru), Capo Sandalo on San Pietro (SP104) and the short hills of Sant'Antioco (Monte S'Arraigraxiu). There are hints of surface damage only on the SP83 near Nebida (2020). No current closures were found.

### Cited Findings
- Genna Bogai: figures as in section 1 — [komoot Highlight 3911409](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/3911409)
- Masua viewpoint after a climb; Sa Rocha steep ramp in the 77 km and 1,690 m loop from Nebida — [komoot Highlight 6073031](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/6073031); [komoot Highlight 4136774](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/4136774)
- SP83 near Nebida: several subsidence spots (2020) — [Comune di Iglesias (PDF)](https://www.comune.iglesias.ca.it/.galleries/doc-consiglio-comunale/interrogazione-Garau-25047-2020-strada-prov.-83-cc-27.07.2020.pdf)
- SP104 Carloforte–Capo Sandalo: about 11 km of asphalt with long climbs — [CAI Cagliari (PDF)](https://www.caicagliari.it/cicloescursionismo/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2018/03/2016_05_15-Descrizione-S.-Pietro.pdf)
- Sant'Antioco: steep descent after Monte S'Arraigraxiu towards Sant'Antioco — [komoot Smarttour 3977980](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/3977980)
- A mountain time trial was held on the SS126 between San Benedetto and Genna Bogai (past event) — [sardegnaturismo node 276356](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/node/276356)
- A search for "SP83 Nebida chiusura frana 2025" found no current closure reports. The only hit is the 2020 PDF — [Comune di Iglesias (PDF)](https://www.comune.iglesias.ca.it/.galleries/doc-consiglio-comunale/interrogazione-Garau-25047-2020-strada-prov.-83-cc-27.07.2020.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because the Giro della Sardegna 2026 rides the Buggerru–Masua–Nebida section, the coast road is very likely paved and passable in 2026 ([sardegnaturismo](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/node/288061)).
- The "SP293 Pantaleo forest road" asked about could not be verified. The CAI sources describe the Pantaleo forest roads as dirt.

### Gaps
- No Strava segment IDs or KOM times could be found (Strava was not indexed and pages could not be opened).
- No current (2024–2026) surface reports for the Sant'Antioco island roads, Giba–Porto Pino, or Santadi–Nuxis. The Climbfinder entry "Masua" is a different Masua near Verona, not relevant.

## 4. Bike shops and rentals, clubs (ASD), organised rides and Granfondos

### Takeaway
Little was found. Sant'Antioco Bike Island offers guided rides and (e-)bike rental. No road-cycling ASD and no Granfondo in the Sulcis could be confirmed. The "Gran Fondo del Sulcis" (Santadi) is a running race.

### Cited Findings
- Sant'Antioco Bike Island, Via Roma 47, Sant'Antioco: guided tours in the Sulcis for cycle tourists and amateurs, also e-bike rental — [Wikivoyage Sant'Antioco](https://it.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sant%27Antioco_(Italia))
- The "Gran Fondo del Sulcis" (6th edition 2012, Santadi) is a 25 km mountain running race by Atletica Santadi, not a cycling event — [Comune di Santadi (PDF)](https://old.comune.santadi.su.it/santadi/resources/cms/documents/gran_fondo_sulcis_6_edizione.pdf)
- Giro della Sardegna 2026: stage 2 runs through the Iglesiente coast and finishes in Carbonia. An earlier edition (28th, year unclear) had a Carbonia–Iglesias stage — [sardegnaturismo node 288061](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/node/288061); [Unione Sarda](https://www.unionesarda.it/sport/ritorna-il-giro-di-sardegnacon-la-classica-sassari-olbia-qyo2ecd1)
- Sardegna Turismo recommends spring and autumn for road riding — [sardegnaturismo node 253626](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/it/node/253626)
- Self-guided multi-day tours by operators (10 Adventures, TourRadar, Level Voyageur, UTracks) include Sant'Antioco and Tratalias–Porto Pino — [10adventures](https://cms.10adventures.com/tour/sardinia-cycling-tour); [TourRadar](https://www.tourradar.com/t/212261); [Level Voyageur](https://www.levelovoyageur.com/en/trip/southern-sardinia-by-bike-with-the-family/)

### Inferences
- For road-bike rental or a club ride, contacting Sant'Antioco Bike Island directly is probably the most promising lead. Clubs could be searched in the FCI regional committee register for Sardinia.

### Gaps
- No bike shops or workshops in Carbonia or Iglesias confirmed by sources. No ASD names with addresses found. No date or official site for the Giro della Sardegna 2026. No Granfondo or Cicloturistica in the Sulcis found.
