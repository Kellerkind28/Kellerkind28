## Traffic: busy or dangerous state roads, quiet SP roads, driver behaviour, 1.5 m passing rule

### Takeaway
No ANAS traffic counts or cyclist-specific reports were found for the SS roads in the southwest. The sources do show that the SS130 (Cagliari to Iglesias) and the SS195 (Cagliari to Pula) carry heavy commuter traffic and have regular serious crashes, and that parts of the SS131 are classified so that bicycles are banned. Tour operators and forum posters consistently send cyclists onto minor roads in the southwest. Italy's 1.5 m passing distance has been law since 14 Dec 2024 (L. 177/2024, art. 148 comma 9-bis CdS), but it applies only "where road conditions allow".

### Cited Findings
- **1.5 m rule:** The Codice della Strada reform (Legge 25 novembre 2024 n. 177, in force since 14 December 2024) amended art. 148. It now requires a lateral distance of at least 1.5 m when overtaking a cyclist, replacing the earlier "adequate distance". — [Everyeye Auto](https://auto.everyeye.it/notizie/sorpassare-ciclista-cosa-codice-strada-distanza-minima-890363.html); [ItaliaOggi](https://www.italiaoggi.it/diritto-e-fisco/giustizia/codice-della-strada-ecco-cosa-cambia-per-i-ciclisti-dal-14-dicembre-yjnafjik)
- **Conditional, not absolute:** Comma 9-bis requires the 1.5 m only "ove le condizioni della strada lo consentano" (where road conditions allow). If there is no room, for example a solid centre line or a narrow carriageway, the driver should wait for a better stretch. A Ministero dell'Interno circular asks for "massima prudenza" and 1.5 m "ove possibile". Earlier drafts with an absolute ban and fines of €162–646 (the "DDL salvaciclisti") were not enacted in that form. — [Everyeye Auto](https://auto.everyeye.it/notizie/sorpassare-ciclista-cosa-codice-strada-distanza-minima-890363.html); [Studio Cataldi (earlier draft)](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/34674-ciclisti-divieto-di-sorpasso-a-meno-di-15-metri-fuori-dai-centri-abitati.asp)
- **SS131 bicycle ban:** ANAS classified the SS131 Serrenti–Sanluri stretch as a "strada extraurbana principale", with a 110 km/h limit. Pedestrians, cyclists and tractors are therefore excluded. The same classification has been requested for other SS131 sections. — [Motociclisti Incolumi](https://www.motociclisti-incolumi.com/sardegna-limiti-velocita-strade-pericolose-nuove-barriere-salva-motociclisti/)
- **SS130 side accesses:** The same article says safety on the SS130 improved after ANAS closed many side accesses. It does not mention a bicycle ban on the SS130. — [Motociclisti Incolumi](https://www.motociclisti-incolumi.com/sardegna-limiti-velocita-strade-pericolose-nuove-barriere-salva-motociclisti/)
- **SS130 "Iglesiente" crashes:** ANAS notices report a fatal crash at km 50.3 near Iglesias (bus and motorcycle, Sept 2024) and a fatal head-on collision at km 53.5 in Iglesias (2 dead, road closed, Nov 2025). Local news reports further crashes and long queues near Assemini and Decimomannu. — [ANAS (Iglesias closure)](https://www.stradeanas.it/it/sardegna-anas-per-incidente-chiusa-temporaneamente-la-statale-130-iglesiente-nel-comune-di-iglesias); [ANAS (Siliqua)](https://www.stradeanas.it/it/sardegna-anas-incidente-traffico-rallentato-su-ss130-%E2%80%9Ciglesiente%E2%80%9D-siliqua-provincia-di-sud-sardegna); [Juorno](https://www.juorno.it/incidente-mortale-sulla-ss130-a-iglesias-due-vittime-e-un-ferito-grave-nello-scontro-frontale/); [L'Unione Sarda (Assemini)](https://www.unionesarda.it/news-sardegna/provincia-cagliari/incidente-sulla-statale-130-ad-assemini-un-ferito-e-traffico-in-tilt-lunghe-code-verso-iglesias-ck228o4m)
- **SS130 congestion:** L'Unione Sarda reports long queues on the SS130 into Cagliari, where Sulcis-Iglesiente commuter traffic adds to the usual load. — [L'Unione Sarda English](https://www.unionesarda.it/en/sardinia/roadworks-on-the-130-have-left-everyone-queuing-even-at-the-entrance-to-cagliari-m4giz065)
- **SS195 "Sulcitana":** A July 2026 crash paralysed traffic toward Cagliari with long queues. — [Casteddu On line](https://www.castedduonline.it/incidente-lungo-la-ss195-traffico-paralizzato-e-lunghe-code-verso-cagliari/)
- **Advice from riders:** A road.cc forum reply on Sardinia says to stay off main roads. The trade-off it notes is more village dogs on quiet roads. — [road.cc forum](https://road.cc/forums/topic/240511-sardinia-route-suggestions)
- **Driver behaviour:** One Tripadvisor poster says three cars stopped to check on them while they fixed a puncture on a quiet road. This is anecdotal. — [Tripadvisor Cycling Sardinia](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g187879-i466-k15487051-Cycling_Sardinia-Sardinia.html)
- **Quiet southwest roads:** Tour operators describe the southwest (Sulcis, Carloforte) as quieter than the northeast, with "more likely to see shepherds tending sheep than cars or trucks", and "quiet roads". — [VBT Sardinia tour](https://www.vbt.com/tours/italy-sardinia-carloforte-sulcis-chia); [Mediterras guide](https://www.mediterras.com/guide-to-cycling-in-sardinia/); [Headwater](https://www.headwater.com/holidays/cycling/italy/C07SA.htm)
- **August beach traffic:** Coastal roads carry beach traffic from mid-morning in August. — [Sardinia Cycling Tours – best time](https://sardiniacyclingtours.com/best-time/)
- **Quiet inland route Cagliari–Sant'Antioco:** A Wikiloc touring track (about 85 km) goes inland via Gutturu Mannu, Terresoli, Santadi, Villaperuccio and Tratalias, avoiding the SS130 and SS195. A comment notes a GPS gap of about 10 km near Santadi. — [Wikiloc Cagliari–San Antioco](https://www.wikiloc.com/bicycle-touring-trails/cagliari-san-antioco-23949458)

### Inferences
- Unverified, from general knowledge: the SS130 between Cagliari/Decimomannu and Iglesias is mostly a four-lane dual carriageway. Together with the reported crash and queue frequency, it should be treated as unsuitable for road bikes. Check the posted signage for any bicycle ban on each stretch.
- Unverified, from general knowledge: the SS195 between Cagliari and Pula carries heavy commuter and beach traffic. The southern SS195 from Pula via Chia to Teulada is two-lane and quieter outside summer. The Costa del Sud panoramic road (SP71, Chia–Teulada) is a scenic classic, but it is crowded with cars in July and August.
- Unverified, from general knowledge: the SS126 between Sant'Antioco, San Giovanni Suergiu, Carbonia and Iglesias is the main Sulcis artery. It is busiest on the isthmus and around Carbonia and Iglesias. Its northern part (Iglesias–Fluminimaggiore–Arbus) is much quieter and is a popular climbing road.
- Unverified, from general knowledge: the SS131 is irrelevant in the southwest except as the main north–south artery, and cyclists should avoid it entirely.
- Because the 1.5 m rule is conditional, close passes on narrow two-lane SS roads remain likely. A rear light or radar and choosing SP roads are more effective protection than the law itself.

## Seasonality: best months, heat, tourist peaks, wind and rain

### Takeaway
The best road-cycling windows are April to June and September to October. Winter is mild and rideable on the coast, but it is the wettest period and windier. July and August bring heat (Cagliari averages 25.6 °C in July, with highs above 30 °C) and beach traffic. The maestrale (NW) is the dominant wind, especially on the west coasts of San Pietro and Sant'Antioco, with scirocco second. The rainy season runs from about late September to early May, with the peak in November.

### Cited Findings
- **Best months:** April to June and September to October, with daytime highs of 20–28 °C. Summer rides should start early. Winter on the coast is "perfectly rideable" but is the wettest part of the year. — [Sardinia Cycling Tours – best time](https://sardiniacyclingtours.com/best-time/); [Mediterras – when to visit](https://www.mediterras.com/when-to-visit-sardinia-for-cycling/)
- **Spring in the southwest:** Spring allows exploring the Sulcis without summer traffic. — [Mediterras guide](https://www.mediterras.com/guide-to-cycling-in-sardinia/)
- **Spring on Carloforte:** Spring is described as the best season for trekking and cycling on Carloforte. The climate is mild because of the sea, and February "already promises spring". — [Carloforte Turismo – info pratiche](https://www.carloforteturismo.it/informazioni-practiche/)
- **Prevailing winds:** The maestrale is the dominant wind, followed by scirocco. The maestrale is very strong on the north and west coasts of Isola di San Pietro. — [Carloforte Turismo](https://www.carloforteturismo.it/informazioni-practiche/); [Wikipedia Isola di Sant'Antioco](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isola_di_Sant'Antioco); [Wikipedia San Pietro Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pietro_Island)
- **Cagliari wind by month:** Mean wind direction is about NNW at 16 km/h in January and November and about SSE at 15 km/h in summer (sea breeze). — [ILMETEO medie climatiche Cagliari](https://www.ilmeteo.it/portale/medie-climatiche/Cagliari)
- **Cagliari Elmas climate, 1981–2010:** Mean January 9.9 °C, July 25.6 °C, year 17.7 °C. Rain 395 mm per year on 61 rain days. November is the wettest month (62.7 mm, 8 days) and July the driest (3.1 mm, 1 day). — [Wikipedia: Stazione meteorologica di Cagliari Elmas](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stazione_meteorologica_di_Cagliari_Elmas)
- **Other annual rain totals:** Other sources give 345–427 mm per year. — [Climi e Viaggi](https://www.climieviaggi.it/clima/italia/cagliari); [Meteomondo](https://meteomondo.it/italia/sardegna/cagliari-2306578/)
- **Wet and dry seasons:** WeatherSpark puts the wetter season at 26 Sept–10 May and the dry season at 10 May–26 Sept. — [WeatherSpark Cagliari Elmas](https://it.weatherspark.com/y/148141/Condizioni-meteorologiche-medie-a-Cagliari-Elmas-Airport-Italia-tutto-l'anno)
- **August heat:** Highs reach about 32 °C in Cagliari in August. Midday heat inland can be "punishing", and coastal breezes help. — [Sardinia Cycling Tours – best time](https://sardiniacyclingtours.com/best-time/)
- **Coastal wind on the Costa del Sud:** Weather on the coast near Teulada can significantly affect riding on the Chia–Porto Pino coastal road. — [komoot road cycling Sud Sardegna](https://www.komoot.com/guide/62409/road-cycling-routes-in-sud-sardegna)

### Inferences
- The south and southwest are among the driest parts of Italy (about 400 mm per year in Cagliari), so rain rarely cancels a ride outside November to February. Wind is the more common limiting factor.
- On Sant'Antioco, San Pietro and the Sulcis coastal plain (including Punta Trettu), a maestrale day means strong NW headwinds on west-facing and northbound legs. It is sensible to plan loops so the return leg runs with the wind.
- Late September to mid-October combines warm sea air, low traffic after the summer, and only occasional early autumn rain. This fits well with the Elmas statistics.

- No traffic-volume curves (ANAS) by month were found to quantify the July and August peak.

## Road surface quality on SP roads and typical hazards

### Takeaway
There are no systematic data. Anecdotes and news show potholes, storm damage and landslides on provincial roads after heavy rain, plus animals on the road (sheep, goats, dogs). Some komoot sections on the Costa del Sud are unpaved. The Sulcis Mountains komoot loop is reported as mostly well paved.

### Cited Findings
- **Sardinian road hazards:** L'Unione Sarda describes "nightmare journeys on Sardinian roads: potholes, construction sites, landslides and animals". Heavy rain caused problems on SP2 between the Sulcis-Iglesiente and Cagliari. Mountain roads see landslides and stray animals. — [L'Unione Sarda English](https://www.unionesarda.it/en/sardinia/nightmare-journeys-on-sardinian-roads-potholes-construction-sites-landslides-and-animals-b685q7du)
- **Dogs:** A road.cc forum reply warns about village dogs on quiet roads. Komoot tips elsewhere in Sardinia mention shepherds' dogs and advise riding slowly and calmly. — [road.cc forum](https://road.cc/forums/topic/240511-sardinia-route-suggestions); [komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/4228047)
- **Sulcis Mountains loop:** Komoot lists a 138 km "Sulcis Mountains" road loop, rated hard, with "mostly well-paved" surfaces. — [komoot road cycling Sud Sardegna](https://www.komoot.com/guide/62409/road-cycling-routes-in-sud-sardegna)
- **Chia–Porto Pino:** The terrain is demanding, with continuous ups and downs. "While most of the path is paved, you might encounter some unpaved sections" (the komoot route uses some side tracks). — [komoot road cycling Sud Sardegna](https://www.komoot.com/guide/62409/road-cycling-routes-in-sud-sardegna)
- **Ex-railway cycle path:** The surface of the Carbonia–Sant'Antioco path was already damaged by vegetation in 2020, and parts needed urgent maintenance. — [La Provincia del Sulcis Iglesiente (2020)](https://www.laprovinciadelsulcisiglesiente.com/2020/05/i-lavori-della-pista-ciclabile-carbonia-santantioco-non-sono-ancora-ultimati-ma-in-alcuni-tratti-richiedono-gia-urgenti-interventi-di-manutenzione/)

### Inferences
- Unverified, general knowledge: wild boar and free-roaming livestock (sheep, goats, occasionally cattle) are common on inland Sulcis roads, for example around Santadi, Nuxis and the Gutturu Mannu forest roads. Wind-blown sand and gravel are common on coastal roads such as the Sant'Antioco isthmus, the Costa del Sud and Porto Pino. Wider tyres (28–32 mm) are a sensible choice.
- Road surface quality varies strongly between SP roads. Check recent komoot or Strava segment comments for the specific roads before riding.

### Gaps
- No official condition survey for Provincia del Sud Sardegna SP roads was found.
- No source specifically addresses wild boar collisions with cyclists in the Sulcis.

## Sardegna Ciclabile / Rete Ciclabile Regionale, Ciclovia della Sardegna, dedicated cycle paths

### Takeaway
The regional cycle network plan (DGR 6/22 of 31 Jan 2017) covers more than 2,000 km and 42–52 itineraries, presented on the Sardegna Ciclabile website and app. It mostly uses existing low-traffic roads, with some dedicated sections. The Sulcis has 4 itineraries under the Piano Sulcis, including the itinerary San Giovanni Suergiu – Sant'Antioco – Calasetta (21.26 km, "Ciclabilità: FACILE", partly on the ex-railway). The Carbonia – San Giovanni Suergiu – Sant'Antioco ex-railway cycle path (about 16 km) was completed in 2020 but has maintenance issues. Separately, the Ciclovia del Cammino Minerario di Santa Barbara is a 9-stage loop from Iglesias through the Sulcis-Iglesiente, including Sant'Antioco.

### Cited Findings
- **Regional plan:** The plan for the regional network of cycling itineraries was approved by DGR 6/22 of 31/01/2017, based on a study by ARST and the University of Cagliari. It covers more than 2,000 km. In 2017 there were 42 itineraries in total, 4 of them under the Piano Sulcis. — [ANCE Sardegna Meridionale (2017)](https://sardegnameridionale.ance.it/2017/02/07/rete-ciclabile-della-sardegna-stanziati-i-primi-15-milioni); [Lavori Pubblici](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/stampa/19130); [L'Unione Sarda](https://www.unionesarda.it/news-sardegna/ecco-la-grande-rete-ciclabile-sarda-oltre-2000-chilometri-di-piste-nell-isola-j0zgagav?amp=1); [greenMe](https://www.greenme.it/mobilita/bici/pista-ciclabile-sardegna/)

### Inferences
- For a road bike, the ex-railway path between Carbonia, San Giovanni Suergiu and Sant'Antioco is a usable traffic-free link if it is paved and maintained. The Sant'Antioco – Calasetta continuation is gravel and unsuitable.
- Most regional "itineraries" are signed routes on ordinary low-traffic roads rather than separated paths. Their GPX files are therefore a useful list of quiet SP and communal roads chosen by the region.
- Unverified, general knowledge: Cagliari has a seafront cycle path along the Poetto beach (about 8 km toward Quartu). It is flat but busy with pedestrians in summer, so it is not a training route.

## Bike rental (road bikes), shops, Calasetta–Carloforte ferry

### Takeaway
Carbon road-bike rental is easiest in Cagliari (from about €20–35 per day) and around Pula and Chia. Several operators deliver bikes to accommodation, including in the Sulcis. Sant'Antioco itself has only a general rental agency, and road bikes are unconfirmed. The Delcomar ferry Calasetta–Carloforte runs year-round, several times per day, crossing in about 30 minutes, with fares from about €4–6 per passenger. The 2024 tariff sheet lists a bicycle supplement of a few euros, but the exact figure is ambiguous in the extracted text.

## Planning tools, GPX collections, clubs and events

### Takeaway
Komoot has curated collections for road cycling in Sud Sardegna and around Sant'Antioco, including a Sant'Antioco ring of about 67–69 km with about 390 m of climbing, a Carloforte island loop of about 42 km rated difficult, and a 138 km Sulcis Mountains loop. The region's Sardegna Ciclabile app has itinerary GPX files. Main 2026 road event: Giro delle Miniere / Gran Fondo delle Miniere (Iglesias, 31 May 2026, 123 km). Sant'Antioco has the "Paradise Island" Gran Fondo (ASD I Due Leoni), but it is probably off-road.
